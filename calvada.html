About


Project Calvada is a vanilla Minecraft forever world project solo developed by Wormwal (Nash). A passion project that is built on-and-off over the years. The first map iteration started with Las Vegia City in 2013. Over the years and several iterations, the project now focuses on creating a detailed Minecraft map, developing several cities and towns, and making each part of the map unique.



The World


Nation of Calvada is a fictional island nation located in the vast Pacific Ocean with a diverse culture of ethnicities, languages, and spiritual beliefs. Calvada is the nation's ideology of unity, a pledge of allegiance. With competing interests developing within her, the promises of a bright nation are mediocre at best. Each province has their own unique short stories. Only waiting to be discovered.
